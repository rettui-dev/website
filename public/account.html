Password

This password is set in the config file. Ask whoever runs the site to change it.

At least 12 characters, and not one of the most common passwords. Signing in elsewhere ends when you change it, and every other known device is forgotten.

When you change it, a pop-up asks for your current password, and for your passkey or a code from your app if two-step sign-in is on.

Every passkey except the one you use to confirm this change is removed. If you confirm with a code or a recovery code, all of them are removed. You can add them again afterwards. Tick this if you think someone else may have added a passkey.
