Two-step sign-in

After your password you also enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (such as Aegis, Google Authenticator or 1Password).

Your password is needed to set it up.

  1. In your authenticator app, add an account and scan this code.
  2. Or type this key in by hand (time-based, 6 digits, 30 seconds):
  3. Enter the code the app shows now.

Your recovery codes

Each code signs you in once, instead of a code from the app, if you lose your phone. They are shown only now: save them somewhere safe.
